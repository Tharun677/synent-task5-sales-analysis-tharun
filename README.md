# Sales Data Analysis

## About the Project

This project was completed as part of my Data Science Internship at Synent Technologies.

The objective of this project was to analyze sales data and identify important business insights. Using the Superstore Sales dataset, I explored revenue trends, product performance, and profit distribution to better understand how different business factors contribute to overall performance.

## Dataset

Superstore Sales Dataset

Source:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vivek468/superstore-dataset-final

## What I Did

- Loaded and explored the sales dataset
- Examined the dataset structure and key business metrics
- Converted date columns into proper datetime format
- Analyzed monthly revenue trends
- Identified top-selling products
- Performed profit analysis by category
- Created visualizations to support findings
- Generated business insights from the data

## Libraries Used

- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Tools Used

- Google Colab
- GitHub

## Visualizations Created

### 1. Monthly Revenue Trend
A line chart was created to analyze how sales revenue changed over time and identify business growth patterns.

### 2. Top 10 Selling Products
A bar chart was used to identify the products generating the highest sales.

### 3. Profit Analysis by Category
A bar chart was created to compare profit generated by different product categories.

## Key Insights

- Revenue showed fluctuations across different months.
- A small number of products contributed significantly to total sales.
- Some product categories generated higher profits than others.
- High sales do not always guarantee high profit.
- Data analysis can help businesses improve decision-making and identify growth opportunities.
